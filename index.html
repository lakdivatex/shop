How to order

Order in 4 easy steps

No account and no online payment. You choose, we confirm everything with you on WhatsApp.

  1. 1

    Choose your style

    Browse the collection and tap any style to see its colours and sizes.

  2. 2

    Pick size & colour

    Select your size and colour, then tap Add to order. Add as many items as you like.

  3. 3

    Send on WhatsApp

    Open your order, add your name and address, then tap Send order on WhatsApp. Your order is already written for you.

  4. 4

    We confirm with you

    We reply on WhatsApp to confirm your items, delivery and how to pay.
